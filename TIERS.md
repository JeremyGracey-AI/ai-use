## Tier 3 — full statement

> **AI-assisted, human-authored.**
>
> Yes, AI is part of how I write. Teaching people to build with AI is my job — it would be odd if it wasn't. Here is the actual split: I build the thing first. AI helps me draft from my own notes and past writing, and helps me build the diagrams. Then I edit until it sounds like me, which usually takes a few passes. I read and approve everything before it goes out.
>
> The projects are mine. The opinions are mine. If something is wrong, that is on me.
>
> I hold my agents to the same standard I'm claiming here: declared behavior must match actual behavior, and someone accountable signs their name to the gap.

Use: jeremygracey.ai/ai, this repo, long-form posts, proposals, talks.

## The AI transparency statement (writing)

Canonical wording for written work — leads WRITING.md and the site page's
written-word section. Use it verbatim wherever a piece of writing needs the
full stance:

> I stand behind every idea and every sentence in every document. Anything I
> publish as writing — essays, posts, arguments, opinions — reflects my own
> thoughts, feelings, and beliefs. AI amplifies them. It does not originate
> them.
> 
> My thesis is simple: pen or speak it first. Prompt and outline it second.
> Then collaborate, iterate, and refine. AI is an extension of intellect, not
> a substitute for it — it aids me in thinking more clearly and more deeply,
> and in staying organized.
